# Background Context
* Cloudflare operates in 348 cities, connects directly to 13,000+ networks, with 95% of Internet-connected population (6B people) within 50 ms of a PoP
* A PoP creates value through lower latency via local processing capacity, and geographically-advantageous failover, not through owning more servers
* Cloudflare generated $2.168B of revenue in 2025, $603.1M of operating cash flow, and $260.6M of free cash flow
* 2025 investment in property/equipment and software was approximately $342.5M, calculated as:
  $603.1M operating cash flow − $260.6M free cash flow = $342.5M
* ***So what?*** Cloudflare can spend, so the question is about whether another PoP produces more customer value than other uses of capital like capacity refresh, resilience, or automation
