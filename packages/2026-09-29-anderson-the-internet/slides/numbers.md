# Numbers to Know

<v-clicks>

* 100 GbE ping-pong, same rack: 1.5µs
* Send 2 KB over commodity network: 2.0µs
* Round trip within same datacenter: 300µs
* Packet round trip CA ↔ NY: 65,000µs or <span text-color-red>65ms</span>
* TLS (secure) Handshake: 3 round trips, or ~200ms
* A mobile page load increase from 1s → 3s increases bounce probability **32%**: <span text-color-green>The Latency Budget</span>
