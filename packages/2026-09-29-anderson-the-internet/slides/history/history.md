# Brief History of The Internet
<v-clicks>

* Started as R&D at ARPA
* In 1969, first information exchanged between UCLA and Stanford
* Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) standardized in 1982
* By 1986, a "network of networks" (ARPANET, MILNET, CSNET, NSFNet)
* Commercial networks launched in 1989 (email)
* WorldWideWeb in 1990
* Commercial exchange (CIX) in 1991
* Fully commercialized in 1995 when NSFNet was decommissioned

</v-clicks>
