# The Difference Between Wi-Fi and Internet

<v-clicks>

* Radios, Antennae, etc = Layer 1
* Wi-Fi Standard (IEEE 802.11) = Layer 2
* The other layers? Recall the "network of networks"
* The typical Wi-Fi Router is responsible for a local network (LAN)
* Requests from your devices negotiate the routing between your local network and the network specified by the DNS address you request
* However, if your outside connection is not working (i.e. your ISP is down), requests on your local network still work
* Likewise, if your router isn't working, your devices cannot route to the public internet
* With this, you now know how to do basic network troubleshooting
