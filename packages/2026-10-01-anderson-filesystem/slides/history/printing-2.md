# The Effect of the Printing Press

<v-clicks>

* A bible took over a year to produce by hand, Gutenberg's press produced 180/year, a <span text-green>**200x increase**</span>
* By 1500, 15-20 million books had been printed in Europe
* Modern newspaper presses can print nearly 1,800 pages/second
* <span text-red>**240,000x increase**</span> in productivity from the first printing press
