# Numbers to Know: File Types

<v-clicks>

* Text Only → few KB
* Documents (text + charts, small images, etc) → few MB
* Photos → few MB
* Short videos → 100s of MB to GB
* Movies/professional video → 10s of GB to TB
* 1 minute of 4K/60Hz video could be equivalent to **thousands** of text files

</v-clicks>
