# Selling Dropbox

<ul>

<li v-click="1">Founded in 2007 when an MIT student continually forgot his flash drive</li>
<li v-click="2">In December 2009, Steve Jobs makes an offer to buy the company for $100 million</li>
</ul>

<h3 v-click="3" mt-2>Your Task</h3>

<ul>
<li v-click="4">You are the founders of Dropbox - will you accept or reject Apple's offer? If you reject the offer, what are you going to do?</li>

<div v-click="5" mt-2>Consider:</div>
<li v-click="6">In its first 7 months, Dropbox had 100k users. Within 18 months, 500k. By October 2009, 2 million</li>
<li v-click="6">Dropbox offers a free tier, with 2GB of storage, and paid plans, with 50GB of storage for $10/month or $100/year</li>
<li text-red v-click="7"><b>Use no additional outside information</b></li>

</ul>
