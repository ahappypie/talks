# What Happened?
* Dropbox rejected Apple's offer of "low nine figures"
* Grew to 50M users in 2011, 100M in 2012, 500M in 2016, and 700M in 2021, with $2.5B in annual revenue today
* Apple launched iCloud, with a competing storage feature, in 2011, which now has 850M users and contributes over $10B to annual revenue
* If Dropbox had taken an all-equity offer from Apple for $100M in 2009, it would be worth about $5.7B today, all else equal
* Dropbox market capitalization is $7.1B
