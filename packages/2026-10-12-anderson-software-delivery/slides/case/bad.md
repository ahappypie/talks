<h1>SLA Defense</h1>
Lean on contractual SLAs and Internal Engineering Roadmaps

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-4 text-xs mt-3">

<div v-click="1">

<h4>Your Pitch</h4>
<ul>
<li>Point to global blended uptime (~99.4%) as SLA compliance</li>
<li>Offer the standard 25% quarterly service credit</li>
<li>Assure the customer your internal roadmap (cell architecture, MySQL sharding) will fix platform stability soon</li>
<li>Push for an annual contract commitment based on contractual concessions</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div v-click="2">

<h4>Impact on Customer</h4>
<ul>
<li>Some or all of their 400 engineers blocked every time something breaks on the platform</li>
<li>Creates delayed product releases, breaks their own client SLAs, burns customer trust</li>
<li>What does a coupon do to mitigate operational failure?</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div v-click="3">

<h4>Financial Reconciliation</h4>
<ul>
<div font-bold>Cost to Customer:</div>
<li>Payroll: $1.44M = 400 devs × $150/hr × 24 hrs</li>
<li>SLA Credit: -$18,750 = 25% × 25% × $300k</li>
<li>Net Loss: <strong>~$1.42M+</strong></li>
</ul>
<ul mt-2>
<div font-bold>Cost to GitHub:</div>
<li>Cash: $18,750</li>
<li>Account Churn: -$300,000 ARR</li>
<li>Net Loss: <strong>$318,750 + reputational damage</strong></li>
</ul>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-2 bg-red-950 border border-red-500 rounded text-xs text-red-200" v-click="4">
<strong>Everybody loses!</strong> Customer spends time and money migrating, GitHub loses this contract and is at risk of losing more within that network
